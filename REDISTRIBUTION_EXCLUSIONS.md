# Third-party materials excluded

- GEO/SRA FASTQ and processed source data: obtain GSE297497 and GSE300977 from NCBI GEO/SRA; their records and contributors remain the authoritative source. Accessions and sample/run assignments are in ACCESSION_MANIFEST.tsv and manifests/.
- Author source workbooks/tables, protein annotation workbooks and paired author/reconstructed per-gene values: not included. GSE297497 source article: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41594-025-01691-6 . Source data must be obtained from the publisher under the applicable terms. Published status alone is not treated as permission to redistribute.
- Reference genomes, UCSC/Ensembl/RefSeq/APPRIS annotations, comparative alignments/CDS caches, sequences and indices: not included. Reference identities/hashes available in retained contracts and provenance; obtain original inputs under provider terms. No missing historical snapshot is invented.
- RNaseFootprinting external repository: https://github.com/zhejilab/RNaseFootprinting . Not bundled or relicensed; attribution here is not an assertion of permission.
- Cutadapt, Bowtie2, TopHat, samtools, HTSeq and other dependencies, runtime environments and vendor binaries: not included; install separately under original licenses. See ENVIRONMENT.md for documentary versions.
- Private manuscripts, audit conversations, credentials, account configuration, correspondence, original local-path manifests and obsolete one-time manuscript packagers: not included.

Project-derived aggregate technical statistics and supplementary result tables are included. No raw counts, enrichment matrices, donor/participant records or third-party per-gene source values are in this release. Original hashes identify private archival versions when release copies have path-only edits. SHA256SUMS.txt verifies current release files; historical manifests must not be used as if their digests described sanitized copies.
